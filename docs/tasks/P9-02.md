TASK P9-02: Financial impact and ROI
PRD: §20   Requirements: FIN01   Depends on: P9-01

CONTEXT
FIN01: cash released is not recurring profit and costs are not deducted twice. §20: ROI for the period = (attributable gross benefits − total costs) ÷ total costs × 100; not used when cost is zero; payback from timed cash flows; finance approves attribution, seasonality, price and workload adjustments and overlaps.

BUILD
1. Benefit categories: demonstrable cash savings, additional contribution margin after variable costs, avoided financing, storage and spoilage costs when realized.
2. Cash released from inventory reduction stored as a separate liquidity measure.
3. Capacity benefits (released staff time) kept non-cash until converted into avoided spend or verified income.
4. Cost categories: implementation, subscription, operating, integration, training.
5. ROI calculation exactly as §20, returning not computable when total cost is zero.
6. Payback period from monthly timed cash flows.
7. Attribution method, adjustments and overlap notes with a finance approval workflow; projected and actual versions.

NON-NEGOTIABLES
- Decimal arithmetic only; one currency per calculation or explicit FX rates.
- Never add inventory principal to recurring annual profit.
- Organization scope comes from the session or API key only; permissions are enforced on the server; every mutating action writes an audit event.
- No secrets or personal data in code, fixtures, logs or messages. User-facing text exists in Arabic and English.

- Zero total cost returns not computable (test_FIN01_*).
- A double-deduction test fails if a cost is counted twice.
- Payback is computed from monthly flows, not from annual ROI.
- Finance approval is required before a figure is labeled approved.
