# Fit assessment questionnaire (PRD §4, §7)

Use with a prospective pilot customer in the 2 to 4 week fit assessment. The goal is to confirm that the problem exists, is measurable and fits a supported operating pattern. Do not assume the problem from the industry alone (§3).

## A. The decision
1. Which planning decision do you want to improve? (weekly production schedule, daily delivery routes, purchasing)
2. How often is it made, by whom, and how long does it take today?
3. What does a bad plan cost you? (late deliveries, overtime, changeovers, stockouts, waste) Can finance put a number on it?

## B. Operations
4. How many lines or machines, shifts, products and orders per week?
5. Are operations batch-based with known routings and alternative lines?
6. Are changeover times different depending on which product follows which?
7. Do materials limit what you can run, and how do you know when they arrive?
8. Which rules are absolute, and which can be broken at a cost?
9. Is any part continuous, chemical, pharmaceutical or heavily regulated? (needs a specialist assessment first, §4)

## C. Systems and data
10. Which systems hold orders, products, routings, stock and actual progress?
11. Can you export a recent sample (4 to 8 weeks) of orders, routings, rates, calendars and stock?
12. Who owns data quality, and who can fix errors quickly?

## D. People and acceptance
13. Who is the operations owner who can approve a new way of planning?
14. Who in finance will agree how benefits are measured?
15. Can you run Veltrix in parallel with your current planning for several weeks?

## E. Result of the assessment
- Fits a supported pattern: yes / no / partly (explain)
- Data sample usable: yes / no (profiler report attached)
- Baseline plan available: yes / no
- Decision: proceed to pilot / not now
